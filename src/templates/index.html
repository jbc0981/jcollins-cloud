My name is Justin Collins, and I've been working in technology and software engineering for more than 20 years. I grew up around computers and was always pretty comfortable figuring out how they worked, although I never really planned on making a career out of it. At one point, I actually wanted to own a bar like Sam Malone from Cheers. Somewhere along the way, life had other plans, and I ended up turning something I was good at into a career.

Over the years, I've worked my way through software development, systems administration, DevOps, cloud infrastructure, and platform engineering. These days, a lot of my work revolves around AWS, Kubernetes, infrastructure as code, CI/CD, GitOps, and developer experience, along with plenty of Python, Go, and whatever other technology happens to be useful for solving the problem in front of me. I've become a big believer in treating infrastructure and application changes the same way we treat code: something that should be versioned, reviewed, repeatable, and easy to understand.

The part of engineering I enjoy most is solving problems. I like figuring out why something isn't working, finding ways to automate repetitive or annoying tasks, and learning new technology just because I want to understand how it works. I'm pretty pragmatic and automation-minded, and I tend to approach problems with the question of "how can we make this better?" rather than just fixing the same problem over and over.

I also enjoy helping other people figure things out. I like taking ownership of a project and seeing it through, but I'm just as happy teaching someone else how to solve the problem themselves. I'm very much a "teach a man to fish" kind of person. If I can build something that makes another engineer's job easier—or teach them something they can use the next time around—that's usually a win in my book.

Outside of work, most of my time revolves around family. With kids involved in things like soccer, band, and marching band, there is usually somewhere I'm supposed to be. When I get some time to myself, I enjoy gaming, tinkering with my home lab, cooking, traveling when I can, and watching a good movie or TV show. I'm a New Orleans Saints fan, an Alabama football fan (Roll Tide!), and a pretty big Dungeons & Dragons nerd.

This site is where I keep some of the things I've built, worked on, and found interesting over the years. If you're interested in the more technical side of what I do, you can find my GitHub and resume below.
